Five rules that save the most money

  1. Compare the amount received. A "zero fee" transfer with a 3% rate margin costs more than a $3 fee at the mid-market rate.
  2. Use the first-transfer promotion — then re-compare for the second transfer.
  3. Send larger, less frequent amounts when fees are flat.
  4. Pay by bank transfer, not card, where card payment adds a surcharge.
  5. Never send money to someone who called you first asking for it — that's the most common remittance scam.

Is it cheaper to send money to Pakistan through a bank or an app?

For most amounts, online transfer services beat traditional bank wires because their exchange margins and fees are lower. Banks can be competitive for very large transfers — compare the PKR received.

What is the mid-market rate?

It is the midpoint between the buy and sell price of a currency on global markets. It is the fairest reference; the further a quote is from it, the more you pay.
