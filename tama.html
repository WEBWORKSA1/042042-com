Tama & western Tokyo · +81 42

In Japan, 042 belongs to Tokyo's western suburbs — the Tama area — and neighbouring Sagamihara in Kanagawa. Over four million people live in this belt of commuter cities, universities and hills.

Which towns use 042?

Japan splits the 042 range into sub-areas. Hachioji, Tachikawa, Fuchu, Chofu, Machida, Kokubunji, Koganei, Hino, Tama city and Sagamihara all sit inside it. Musashino and Mitaka use 0422, and Ome uses 0428 — those still begin with 042 on caller ID.

How to dial a Tama number

  • Inside Japan: dial all 10 digits, e.g. 042-623-1234.
  • From abroad: your exit code, then 81 42 and the 7 digits, dropping the 0. Mobile format: +81 42 623 1234.
  • Mobiles start 070, 080 or 090. IP phones start 050. Free-dial numbers start 0120 or 0800.
  • Premium ranges such as 0570 (Navi Dial) and 0990 cost more — be careful returning missed calls.

Japan's "special fraud" scams

Japan's National Police Agency counted ¥71.8 billion lost to special-fraud phone scams in 2024, up 58.6% on the year, across about 21,000 cases. Older residents of suburban areas like Tama are frequent targets, and foreign residents are increasingly hit by English- and Chinese-language robocalls.

  • "Ore ore" (it's me) calls. A "son" or "grandson" has lost money or caused an accident and needs cash today.
  • Fake police or bank staff at the door. They collect your cash card "for safekeeping".
  • Refund fraud. You're owed a medical or tax refund and must go to an ATM to "receive" it. ATMs can't receive refunds.
  • Fake carrier or utility calls about unpaid fees, often as automated voice messages pressing you to "press 1".
  • Fake embassy or immigration calls to foreign residents, threatening visa problems.

Living in the Tama area

Rents are noticeably lower than central Tokyo while staying within an hour of Shinjuku by train, which is why students, young families and remote workers move here. Key tips:

  • Check the station's line, not just the distance — a rapid service can halve the commute.
  • Many landlords need a Japanese phone number and a guarantor company.
  • Machida and Sagamihara straddle the Tokyo–Kanagawa border; ward services differ.

Tama FAQ

Is 042 a Tokyo area code?

Yes. 042 covers the Tama area of western Tokyo and neighbouring Sagamihara. Central Tokyo uses 03.

What is the difference between 03 and 042?

03 is central Tokyo's 23 wards. 042 is the western suburbs. Both are Tokyo-area landlines; from abroad they are +81 3 and +81 42.

I missed a call from abroad starting +81 42. Should I call back?

If you don't know anyone in Tokyo, don't. Send a message or search the number first. Calling back unknown international numbers can be expensive.

What time is it in Hachioji?

Japan Standard Time, UTC+9, all year round.
