What does 042 mean?

A dialing code in three countries, a joke about the meaning of life, a retired jersey and a pun in Japanese. Here's everything the number carries.

Japanese wordplay

In Japan, numbers are read as syllables in a practice called goroawase. Because 0 can be read "o", 4 "shi" and 2 "ni", the digits 04 spell "oshi" — the word fans use for their favourite idol, character or performer. Phone numbers, prices and dates in Japan are often chosen for these readings.

Chinese and East Asian superstition

The digit 4 sounds like the word for death in Mandarin (sì) and Japanese (shi), so many buildings skip fourth floors and room 4. The digit 2, on the other hand, suggests pairs and balance and is considered lucky. 042 combines the most avoided digit with an auspicious one.

Pop culture and programmers

42 became famous as the comic "answer to the ultimate question of life, the universe and everything" in a 1979 British science-fiction novel. Ever since, programmers and science fans have used 42 as a running joke, an easter egg and a default test value.

Sport

Number 42 is retired across every Major League Baseball team in honour of Jackie Robinson, who broke the sport's colour barrier in 1947. Every April 15, all players wear 42.

Mathematics

  • 42 = 2 × 3 × 7, and is the product of two consecutive numbers (6 × 7), which makes it a pronic number.
  • 42 is the fifth Catalan number: there are 42 ways to cut a seven-sided polygon into triangles.
  • 042042 = 42 × 1001 = 2 × 3 × 7 × 7 × 11 × 13. Writing any three-digit number twice is the same as multiplying it by 1001.

Numerology

Popular numerology adds the digits: 0 + 4 + 2 = 6, associated with home, care and responsibility. Repeated as 042042, enthusiasts read it as a doubled message. These readings are cultural tradition, not science.

Telephony

042 is one of the few area codes used in three countries at once — Lahore in Pakistan, Daejeon in South Korea and the Tama area of Tokyo. The 0 is a domestic trunk prefix in all three, which is why from abroad the code becomes simply 42.

Is 042 a lucky number?

It depends on the culture. In China and Japan the 4 is considered unlucky, while in numerology the total 6 is seen as harmonious. In Japanese fan culture, 04 reads as "oshi", which is affectionate.

Why is 42 the answer to everything?

It comes from a 1979 science-fiction comedy novel in which a supercomputer gives 42 as the answer to life, the universe and everything — without anyone knowing the question. The author said he chose it as an ordinary, unremarkable number.
