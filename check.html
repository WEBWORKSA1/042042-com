How the check works

  1. We read the format. Country code, the 42 area code and the number of digits after it tell us which numbering plan the number fits.
  2. We look for risk signals. International premium ranges, ranges used in one-ring "call-back" fraud and spoofing patterns raise the score.
  3. We add reports. If you or others on this device reported the number, those reports count too.
  4. You decide. The score is guidance. A clean score never means a caller is genuine — caller IDs can be faked.

    Number checker FAQ

    Can you tell me the name of the person who called?

    No. We don't publish names or addresses linked to phone numbers. That protects people whose numbers are spoofed by scammers, and it keeps the site within privacy laws in all three countries.

    Why does my number match more than one country?

    A local number written as 042 plus 7 or 8 digits can be valid in two countries at once. Ask yourself whether the call arrived as international — that settles it.

    I already shared a code or paid. What now?

    Call your bank immediately using the number on the back of your card, then follow the steps on our scam shield page for your country.
