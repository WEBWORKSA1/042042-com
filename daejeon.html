How to dial a Daejeon number

  • Inside Daejeon: dial the 7 or 8-digit number.
  • From elsewhere in Korea: 042 then the number.
  • From abroad: your exit code, then 82 42 and the number, dropping the 0. Mobile format: +82 42 123 4567.
  • Mobiles start 010 (+82 10 from abroad). Internet phones start 070 — common for businesses and, unfortunately, for spam.

Voice phishing in Korea: what it sounds like

Korean voice-phishing (보이스피싱) losses reached ₩952.5 billion in 2024 including smishing, and kept climbing in 2025. Foreign residents and students are frequent targets because scammers exploit unfamiliarity with Korean institutions.

  • Fake prosecutor or police. "Your bank account was used in a crime. Move your money to a safe account for investigation." No Korean authority moves money this way.
  • Loan refinancing. A cheaper loan offer that requires installing an app — the app hijacks your phone and redirects your calls.
  • Smishing. Texts about a wedding invitation, obituary, parcel or traffic fine with a link. Don't tap it.
  • Family emergency. A message from a "child" with a broken phone asking for money or gift cards.
  • Investment rooms. Chat groups promising guaranteed stock or crypto returns.

Moving to or studying in Daejeon

Daejeon attracts researchers, engineers and international students to its universities and government research institutes. Practical first steps for newcomers:

  1. Get a Korean phone number. Many services, including banking and delivery apps, need identity verification through a Korean mobile number.
  2. Register your residence. Apply for your residence card after arrival; most things depend on it.
  3. Open a bank account. Bring your residence card and passport; ask for the English-language app.
  4. Sort health insurance. Long-stay residents enrol in national health insurance; private cover helps in the gap.

Daejeon FAQ

What is the area code for Daejeon?

042 inside Korea. From abroad dial +82 42 followed by the local number.

Why do I get calls from 070 numbers in Korea?

070 is the range for internet phones. Many legitimate businesses use it, but it is also used by telemarketers and scammers because it is cheap and easy to spoof.

Someone from "the prosecutor's office" called me. What should I do?

Hang up. Call 112 yourself and ask whether there is a case. Real investigators never ask you to transfer money or install apps.

What time is it in Daejeon?

Korea Standard Time, UTC+9, all year round.
