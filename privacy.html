Privacy policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

What we collect

  • Numbers you check are processed in your browser and are not sent to us.
  • Number reports and form submissions (name, email, phone, message and anything else you type) are sent to us when you press submit. Forms are delivered by a third-party form-processing service.
  • Device storage: we store your theme, cookie choice and your own number reports in your browser's local storage.
  • Usage data such as pages viewed, collected by advertising and analytics partners through cookies.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google's Ads Settings (adssettings.google.com) or at aboutads.info. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, a consent message lets you choose how cookies are used.

How we use information

  • To answer your message or fulfil your quote request.
  • With your consent, to pass a quote request to up to three relevant providers.
  • To send the newsletter you subscribed to.
  • To review number reports and keep the site safe.

We never sell personal information as a list.

Retention

Messages and leads are kept for up to 24 months, newsletter data until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR, UK GDPR, Canada's PIPEDA and Quebec's Law 25, California's CCPA, Korea's PIPA, Japan's APPI and Pakistan's applicable laws), you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent. Use the contact form and choose "Privacy / data request".

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We'll update this page when practices change and revise the date above.
