How to dial a Lahore number

  • Inside Lahore: dial the 8-digit number only, e.g. 3575 1234.
  • From elsewhere in Pakistan: 042 then the number, e.g. 042 3575 1234.
  • From abroad: your exit code, then 92 42 and the number — drop the 0. From a mobile, +92 42 3575 1234 works everywhere.
  • Business "111" numbers: many Lahore companies use universal access numbers written 042-111-XXX-XXX.
  • Mobiles never use 042. They start 03, e.g. 0300, 0321, 0333, 0345 (+92 3XX from abroad).

Scam calls Lahore residents report most

  • Fake prizes. A caller says you won a car, phone or cash in a TV game show or SIM lucky draw, then asks for a "tax" or "delivery" fee.
  • Fake government benefits. Callers pose as a welfare programme and ask for your CNIC number and the code sent to your phone.
  • Bank verification. "Your card is blocked, confirm your PIN or OTP." Pakistani banks don't ask for these by phone.
  • WhatsApp takeover. "I sent you a 6-digit code by mistake, please forward it." That code is your WhatsApp account.
  • Relative in trouble abroad. Overseas families are targeted with "your son was arrested, send money now" calls.

Calling and sending money to Lahore

Overseas Pakistanis sent a record $38.3 billion home in FY2025, up 27% on the year. The biggest senders were Saudi Arabia ($9.3B), the UAE ($7.9B), the UK ($5.9B) and the EU ($4.5B). On a typical transfer, the difference between the cheapest and most expensive option is often several percent — real money on a monthly family transfer.

  • Compare the amount received in PKR, not the fee. Exchange-rate margins usually cost more than the fee.
  • Mobile-wallet payout (JazzCash, Easypaisa) is often fastest for family in Lahore without a bank account.
  • First-transfer promotions are real savings, but check the rate after the promo ends.
  • For calls, app-to-app is free; bundles to Pakistani landlines and mobiles cost a few cents a minute.

Lahore FAQ

What is the area code for Lahore?

042 inside Pakistan. From abroad, dial +92 42 followed by the 8-digit number.

What time is it in Lahore?

Lahore uses Pakistan Standard Time, UTC+5, all year. Our dialing calculator shows the live time.

Is a call from +92 42 a scam?

Usually not — it's a normal Lahore landline. Be suspicious if the caller asks for your CNIC, a one-time code, a PIN or an urgent payment.

How do I report a scam call in Pakistan?

Call the national cyber-crime helpline 1799 and file a complaint with the PTA online. Report card fraud to your bank first.
