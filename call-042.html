The three parts of an international number

  1. Exit code — tells your phone company you're dialling abroad. It's 011 from the US and Canada, 00 from most of Europe, the Gulf and South Asia, 010 from Japan and 001 from Korea. On a mobile, the + sign replaces it.
  2. Country code — 92 for Pakistan, 82 for South Korea, 81 for Japan.
  3. Area code without the 0 — 42. The leading 0 is a domestic prefix; dialling it from abroad is the most common reason a call to 042 fails.
  4. Local number — 8 digits in Lahore, 7 or 8 in Daejeon, 7 in Tama.

Why does my call to 042 not connect?

Most often the 0 was included after the country code. Dial +92 42…, not +92 042…. Also check the local number length for the region.

Is it free to call 042 numbers?

Calls to landlines abroad are billed by your carrier unless you use an internet calling app or a bundle. App-to-app calls are free over Wi-Fi.
