Questions people ask about 042

Where is area code 042?

042 is used in three places: Lahore in Pakistan (+92 42), Daejeon in South Korea (+82 42), and the Tama area of western Tokyo plus neighbouring Sagamihara in Japan (+81 42). The leading 0 is only dialled inside each country.

How can I tell which country a 042 call came from?

Look at the full caller ID. International calls usually show +92, +82 or +81 before the 42. Without a country code, count the digits after 042: Lahore landlines have 8, Daejeon 7 or 8, Tama 7.

Is a call from 042 a scam?

Most 042 calls are ordinary landline calls. Be careful if the caller asks for a one-time code, a card PIN, an urgent transfer, or claims to be police, a bank or a government programme. Caller IDs can also be faked, so a real-looking 042 number proves nothing.

Do you store the numbers I check?

No. The checker runs in your browser. Only reports you choose to submit are sent to us for review.
